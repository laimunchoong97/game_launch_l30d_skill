# Source guide

## Highest-confidence sources

- Official publisher or developer announcements for confirmed milestones.
- Public investor reports for reported units or revenue.
- Steam public endpoints for metadata, price, reviews, and current players.
- Steam reviews for buyer reception, with review-bombing and selection bias checks.

## Medium-confidence sources

- SteamDB for followers, price history, release state, packages, and player context.
- Steam Charts for player-count history.
- OpenCritic and Metacritic for professional reception, with user-review bias checks.
- Circana, GSD, and Newzoo for market benchmarks when accessible.
- VG Insights, Gamalytic, SteamSpy, and PlayTracker for estimates only.

## Attention and qualitative sources

- Reddit, game-specific subreddits, and Steam forums.
- YouTube trailers, reviews, previews, and comments.
- TwitchTracker and SullyGnome.
- Google Trends.
- X, TikTok, and other social platforms.
- Gaming publications and creator coverage.

## Restrictions

No public source reliably exposes a reseller's key sales, regional key demand, private Steam wishlists, or exact sales for most games. These must be labeled unavailable or estimated. Do not use undocumented SteamDB APIs, Steam tokens, or private publisher data.
